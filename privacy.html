Privacy Policy

IRIS · Last updated 30 August 2026

Polska wersja poniżej. The English text governs.

IRIS is a tool that lets an Instagram creator or business automatically reply to comments on their own posts and turn commenters into email subscribers. This policy explains what data IRIS handles, why, and how to have it deleted.

Who we are

IRIS is operated by the individual identified in the Meta App Dashboard as this application's contact. Questions and requests: privacy@irisapp.pl.

What we collect

Two distinct groups of people are involved, and the data differs for each.

If you connect your Instagram account to IRIS (our customer)

DataWhy
Instagram account ID, username, account typeIdentify which account a campaign belongs to
Access token issued by MetaMake API calls on your behalf; stored encrypted
Your posts and their comment countsLet you pick which post a campaign runs on
Campaign settings you createKeywords, reply text, and related configuration
Delivery logsShow you what was sent and diagnose failures

If you comment on one of our customers' posts

DataWhy
Your comment textMatch it against the campaign's keywords
Your Instagram-scoped user ID and usernameAddress the automated reply to you
Record of messages sent to youPrevent duplicate messages and honour Meta's one-reply-per-comment rule
Email address, only if you choose to provide oneAdd you to the creator's mailing list, if you opt in

We do not collect your Instagram password, your private messages with anyone other than the connected account, your contacts, or your browsing activity.

Where the data comes from

Entirely from Meta's official Instagram Graph API, using access you granted through Instagram's own permission screen. IRIS does not scrape Instagram, does not automate the Instagram app or website, and never asks anyone for their Instagram password.

How we use it

Only to operate the service: to detect matching comments, send the replies our customer configured, show delivery results, and keep the service secure and working. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it to train machine-learning models or for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only with infrastructure providers needed to run the service — hosting, database, and email delivery — each acting on our instructions. We also share data back with Meta when sending a reply, which is inherent to how the product works. We disclose data to authorities only where legally required.

How long we keep it

  • Access tokens — deleted at once when you disconnect the account in IRIS or remove IRIS in your Instagram settings; otherwise kept until the token expires (Meta issues 60-day tokens), then deleted promptly.
  • Comment and delivery records — up to 24 months, so customers can see historical performance.
  • Everything tied to an account — deleted within 30 days of that account being disconnected or a deletion request being completed.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you may request access to your data, correction, deletion, a portable copy, or restriction of processing, and you may object to processing. Email privacy@irisapp.pl; we respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Deleting your data

See the Data Deletion instructions.

International transfers

Data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States, under appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Children

IRIS is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child's data has reached us, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected in the date at the top of this page. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.

Skąd pochodzą dane

W całości z oficjalnego Instagram Graph API firmy Meta, na podstawie dostępu przyznanego na własnym ekranie uprawnień Instagrama. IRIS nie pobiera danych z Instagrama metodą scrapingu, nie automatyzuje aplikacji ani strony internetowej Instagrama i nigdy nie prosi nikogo o hasło do Instagrama.

Jak je wykorzystujemy

Tylko do działania usługi: do wykrywania pasujących komentarzy, wysyłania odpowiedzi skonfigurowanych przez naszego klienta, pokazywania wyników dostarczenia oraz dbania o bezpieczeństwo i sprawne działanie usługi. Nie sprzedajemy danych osobowych i nie używamy ich do trenowania modeli uczenia maszynowego ani do reklamy.

Komu je przekazujemy

Tylko dostawcom infrastruktury potrzebnej do działania usługi: hostingu, bazy danych i dostarczania e-maili, z których każdy działa na nasze polecenie. Przekazujemy też dane z powrotem do Meta przy wysyłaniu odpowiedzi, co wynika z samego sposobu działania produktu. Ujawniamy dane organom władzy tylko tam, gdzie wymaga tego prawo.

Jak długo je przechowujemy

  • Tokeny dostępu: usuwamy od razu, gdy odłączysz konto w IRIS albo usuniesz IRIS w ustawieniach Instagrama; w innym razie zostają do wygaśnięcia tokenu (Meta wydaje tokeny ważne 60 dni) i potem są niezwłocznie usuwane.
  • Zapisy komentarzy i dostarczeń: do 24 miesięcy, aby klienci mogli widzieć historyczne wyniki.
  • Wszystko, co jest powiązane z kontem: usuwamy w ciągu 30 dni od odłączenia tego konta albo od zakończenia realizacji żądania usunięcia.
