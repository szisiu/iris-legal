Data Deletion Instructions

IRIS · Last updated 30 August 2026

You can have all data IRIS holds about you deleted. Choose whichever route applies.

If you connected your Instagram account to IRIS

Disconnecting removes your data. Either:

  • In IRIS — open Settings and choose Disconnect Instagram. This revokes the stored access token and schedules deletion of your account data.
  • In Instagram — open your profile, then Settings and activity → Apps and websites, find IRIS, and remove it. This immediately revokes our access.

Removing the app in Instagram stops all future access, but to have data already stored erased, also email us using the form below — or use the in-app disconnect, which does both.

If you commented on someone's post and received a message

You were messaged by the creator whose post you commented on, using IRIS. To have your data erased, email us with the details below.

How to request deletion by email

Send an email to szkolkak@wp.pl with the subject Data deletion request, including:

  • Your Instagram username
  • Whether you connected an account to IRIS, or commented on someone else's post
  • If applicable, the post or creator involved

We may ask you to confirm the request from an address or account associated with the data, so that we do not delete someone else's information on a stranger's say-so.

What gets deleted

  • Access tokens and the connection to your Instagram account
  • Your campaigns, keywords and configured reply content
  • Stored comment records and delivery logs relating to you
  • Any email address collected through IRIS on your behalf or from you

How long it takes

We acknowledge within 7 days and complete deletion within 30 days. Backups are purged on their normal rotation, no later than 90 days. We may retain the minimum needed to meet a legal obligation, or a record that a deletion request was made and honoured.
